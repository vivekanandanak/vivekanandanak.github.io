Dr. Vivekanandan A.K. is a materials scientist, mechanical engineer, researcher, and educator with more than 11 years of academic and research experience across India and Taiwan. Since November 2025, he has served as a Postdoctoral Researcher in Materials Science at National Tsing Hua University, Taiwan.

His current work examines high-entropy materials, advanced additive manufacturing, and the reliability of fine-pitch copper redistribution lines. He studies electromigration, Joule heating, void nucleation, grain-boundary degradation, and oxidation using materials characterization and coupled thermal-mechanical analysis.

He earned his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the National Taiwan University of Science and Technology, specializing in nanomaterials. Previously, he served as an Assistant Professor of Aeronautical Engineering in India, where he taught aircraft structures, strength of materials, composite materials, and engineering mechanics while supervising student projects.

Semiconductor reliability
